Project Tracker

Project Tracker is a simple personal Android app for tracking projects, team members and daily work hours, and for creating a monthly hours report as an Excel (.xlsx) file.

Features

  • Manage projects and team members
  • Record work entries with date, in-time and out-time
  • Create and share a monthly report as an .xlsx file
  • Your data is stored in your own Google Drive, not on our servers

The app signs in with your Google account and only accesses a spreadsheet it creates itself in your Google Drive.
