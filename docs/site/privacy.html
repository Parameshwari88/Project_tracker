Privacy Policy

App: Project Tracker (Android)   Last updated: 2026-11-19

What the app does

Project Tracker lets you record projects, team members and work hours, and create a monthly report as an Excel file.

Information the app handles

  • Google account sign-in: the app uses Google Sign-In to identify your account (name and email address are used only to show who is signed in).
  • Your tracker data: projects, members (name, phone, role) and work entries that you type in.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored only in a Google Sheet named "Project Tracker DB" in your own Google Drive. The app requests the Google Drive scope drive.file, which allows it to access only files that the app itself created. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

The developer has no server and does not receive, collect, store or have access to your data.

Reports

Monthly reports are created on your phone, saved temporarily, and passed to the Android share sheet so you can send them where you choose. The temporary file is deleted afterwards.

Sharing and third parties

The app does not sell or share your data, and contains no advertising or analytics. It communicates only with Google services (sign-in, Google Sheets and Google Drive) on your behalf. Google's own privacy policy applies to that processing.

Deleting your data

You can delete the "Project Tracker DB" sheet from your Google Drive at any time, and revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
